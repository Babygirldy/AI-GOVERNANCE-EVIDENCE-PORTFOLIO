# AI Governance, Law & Technology Risk — Evidence Portfolio

**Practical AI governance | Responsible AI | UK technology law | Technology risk and assurance**

This public portfolio records my development through applied AI governance case studies, regulatory research, risk assessments and reasoned professional decisions. It is organised to show **what I investigated, what I produced, what evidence supports my judgement and what remains unfinished**.

> **Authorship and evidence note:** These are fictional learning scenarios, not client work or regulator-issued compliance approvals. AI assistance was used to structure and edit accompanying explanatory documents. The original Day 02 Excel workbook is preserved as evidence of learner entries. All legal findings should be independently checked against authoritative current sources.

## Skills this portfolio is designed to demonstrate

- AI system intake and intended-purpose mapping.
- Identification of fairness, human oversight, data protection and technology risks.
- Legal and regulatory research with stated jurisdiction and uncertainties.
- Vendor-evidence requests and risk-based hold/go recommendations.
- Traceable documentation, risk-control ownership and continuous improvement.

## Working method

**Scope → verify sources → assess risks → request evidence → recommend controls → document decisions → monitor and reflect.**

Every case study distinguishes original workbook answers, training-template examples, assistant-curated summaries and independently verified findings.
