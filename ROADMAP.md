## Standards for every case study

1. State facts and assumptions separately.
2. Keep original learner-created materials.
3. Cite primary laws and trustworthy technical references.
4. Label missing and unverified evidence explicitly.
5. Explain decisions and residual risk.
6. Commit each meaningful revision so the improvement is traceable.
