## Executive summary

Northstar Talent UK is considering a supplier-provided AI CV-ranking system to support high-volume recruitment. The supplier says the ranking model draws on historical hiring decisions. Independent validation, fairness testing, privacy assessment, and documented human-review controls have **not** been supplied in this scenario.

**Learner's recorded recommendation:** **HOLD — no production deployment pending satisfactory review of essential evidence and controls.** This is an *exercise recommendation*, not an actual legal or corporate approval decision.

## Central governance insight

A vendor's claim of strong accuracy is not proof of fair, safe or lawful use. Before deployment, the organisation should determine how applicants are affected, obtain meaningful assurance evidence, assess applicable UK data protection and equality obligations, identify genuine human oversight and formally record accountability.
