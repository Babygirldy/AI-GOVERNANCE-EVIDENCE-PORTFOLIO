## 2. Assessment of the response (assistant feedback)

| Learner's line of enquiry | What it shows | Evidence questions to develop |
|---|---|---|
| Were tests done? | Appropriate concern about supplier assurance | Which test data, independent validation methods, metrics, false-negative rate, job families and sample sizes? |
| Risk if some names are exempted | Concern about uneven treatment and discriminatory impact | Does 'exempted' mean names removed from CVs, certain applicants screened out or different processes? Could proxies still cause disparate outcomes? |
| Who is liable if errors occur? | Accountability and redress awareness | Identify obligations of the employer and supplier under applicable law and contract; clarify applicant challenge and correction mechanisms. Liability **cannot be assigned categorically without facts**. |
| Will there be updates? | Lifecycle governance and change-control awareness | Request version history, update approval, retesting, drift monitoring and rollback procedures. |

**What remains:** Explicitly explain the harm caused by false negatives and false positives in the ranking, propose a specific safeguard and state how supplier evidence would affect the R-04 rating.

## 3. Assistant-edited professional example (not a verbatim learner statement)

> Before recommending deployment of HireSort AI, I would request independent performance-testing evidence and ask how the claimed accuracy was measured, which applicant populations and job families were included and how often qualified applicants were incorrectly ranked. I would investigate whether names or other variables cause unequal treatment or proxy discrimination. I would also clarify supplier and employer responsibilities for errors and establish accessible correction and challenge procedures. Finally, I would review the vendor's update, change-control and monitoring arrangements. I would recommend withholding deployment approval while material performance, fairness and oversight evidence remains absent.

**Authorship rule:** Do **not** claim this model paragraph was independently written by the learner. Learner review, source checking and revision remain outstanding.

## 4. Next guided prompt — one small task

**Scenario:** The supplier claims **90% accuracy** but will not disclose how many qualified candidates its AI incorrectly ranks below the interview threshold.

Answer in **two or three of your own sentences**:

1. What is the **harm** for a genuinely qualified applicant who is ranked too low?
2. What **one safeguard or test** should the employer demand before live use?

This will let us test whether the learner can connect **risk → harm → safeguard → evidence** and finalise R-04 more independently.
