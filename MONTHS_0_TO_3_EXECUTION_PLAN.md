# Months 0–3 — Applied AI Governance execution plan

**Plan drafted:** 7 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Build demonstrable skills and a truthful public evidence portfolio while paid AIGP training/exam is deferred.  
**Status:** Learning roadmap, not proof of task completion. Existing Day 01–02 work remains credited to its actual dates.

## What 'done' means
A topic is **studied** when a dated learning entry explains it. A skill is **demonstrated** when it produces an original, reviewable output. A case is **reviewed** when its facts, unknowns, sources, risk/control logic, and decision are critically examined. None of these labels substitutes for independent audit or professional sign-off.

## Start here: one guided session at a time

### Session A — Day 02, R-04
1. Read [R-04 worked example](projects/day-02-ai-recruitment/risk-register-expansion-worked-example.md).
2. Find R-04 in the **original workbook copy** (sheet `03 Risk Register`).
3. Answer in your own words: *What is the harm? What control would help? What evidence would change my judgement?*
4. Put your answer into the **new working copy**, not the preserved original. Date the entry.
5. Mark 'Needs review' if you cannot yet defend the score.

### Session B — Day 02, R-05 to R-07
Repeat the same three questions for transparency, supplier security and model updates. Compare your answers with the source guide; revise before status is 'Demonstrated'.

### Session C — Day 03 case intake
Read the [hospital system inventory](projects/day-03-healthcare-ai/ai-system-inventory.md), then the [risks](projects/day-03-healthcare-ai/risk-register.md). Explain the alert-to-clinician workflow, then inspect [impact assessment](projects/day-03-healthcare-ai/impact-assessment.md).

## Portfolio integrity
- Keep original uploaded spreadsheets unmodified; revise a separate version.
- Label assistant-prepared examples as examples, not learner work.
- Never claim risk controls have passed tests without actual results.
- Separate legal sources from proposed policy and scenario assumptions.
- No real applicant, patient or confidential employer information.
- Paid AIGP certification can remain paused; practical learning continues.
